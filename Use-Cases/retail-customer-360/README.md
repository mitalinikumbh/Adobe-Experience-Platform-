# Fashion Brand — Customer 360

## Overview

This use case follows a fictional fashion retail brand as a customer signs up, browses products, and makes a purchase.

The goal is to understand how Adobe Experience Platform (AEP) can help structure customer data and bring together customer information and behavioural events.

## What We Will Learn

- **XDM:** How customer and event data are structured.
- **Schemas and datasets:** How data models are defined and records are organised.
- **Identity Service:** How customer identities are represented and connected.
- **Real-Time Customer Profile:** How profile-enabled data contributes to a unified customer view.
- **Audience segmentation:** How customer groups can be defined using relevant data.

## Learning Approach

Each concept will be explained through the same business scenario, with simple examples, technical artefacts, diagrams, and links to official Adobe documentation.

## Disclaimer

This is an independent educational project based on a fictional business scenario. Please also refer the adobe experience platform offical documentation.
